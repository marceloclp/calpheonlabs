This site pairs every table decoder with a live confidence ledger. Each page
separates what the bytes prove from what the fields are believed to mean, and
keeps missing evidence visible instead of turning it into an implicit claim.

- [Browse every table confidence ledger](/tables)
- [Read the decoder and confidence methodology](/methodology)

The binary capture and full snapshot comparison are the decoding authorities.
Historical decoders, offset directories, archived databases, and external
sites may supply corroborating evidence, but they are not runtime dependencies.
