The client calculates the published probabilities as:

- common: `eventRate × (1 - rareConditional) × (1 - specialConditional)`
- special: `eventRate × (1 - rareConditional) × specialConditional`
- rare: `eventRate × rareConditional`

Channel zero's conditional rate is always 1,000,000 and identifies the common branch; it is not multiplied into the displayed common formula.
